**fig_dist_breakeven.** Cost of the front end per full frame against the number of frames n that share one search of the distance (frames per mounting, log scale). Curves: the blind search of the paper (AF once/session) and the cheap search (cheap AF once/session) done once and reused by the n frames; the cost falls as 1/n. Horizontal lines: the three learned focal stacks (5, 7 and 9 distances), which cost the same whatever n, and two conventional references, the reconstruction at the nominal distance and the cheap search on every frame; each is named on the right, joined to its line by a thin connector. Where a curve crosses a stack line (open circles, with the value of n*), focusing once per mounting becomes cheaper than the stack: below n* the stack is cheaper, above it the conventional flow is. A grey circle marks a crossover the measurement does not resolve (moving the two times that n* subtracts by 3 % in opposite directions flips the sign of their difference, label "≈": the stack costs the same as one reconstruction, or changes n* by more than a factor 2, label: the range). (a) One GPU (minimum time over the repetitions). (b) One CPU core (median). All times are sums of measured stage times.

**fig_dist_cost_f1.** Cost against accuracy on full frames of the sensor. Each point is a flow; up and to the left is better. Time per frame on one GPU (log scale), as sums of measured stage times; the flows that reuse a distance are charged a session of 100 frames. Accuracy: open-set F1 on the boxes found by the detector. (a) Distance fixed in each session (the frames of the paper), cost of the front end only. (b) The same frames, cost end to end, with the detector. (c) Distance changing at every frame by 5 %, front end only. (d) The same, end to end.

**fig_dist_tilt.** Open-set F1 end to end on full frames against the tilt of the slide (bottom axis, degrees). The top axis gives the change of distance this tilt makes at the edge of the frame, 3.2 mm x tan(tilt), in micrometres. Each line is a flow: reconstructions at the nominal distance, after a global autofocus, with per-object focus over a wide range, the cheap autofocus, and the three focal stacks (legend below the axis). Single panel; mean over the five repetitions.

**fig_dist_sets.** Open-set F1 of every flow (columns) in every set of test windows (rows): the bench, the distance changing between sessions (2 and 5 %), at every image (2 and 5 %) or drifting 0.5 % per image, strong shot noise and a narrow beam. Models, galleries and thresholds are those of the base set; mean over the repetitions. Darker is better.

**fig_dist_sweep.** The validation sweep of the 256 candidate searches of the distance: share of validation images with a gross error (above 1 % of the nominal distance; symmetric log scale) against the cost of the search in reconstructions of the whole window (log scale). Grey: every candidate. Highlighted and labelled: the blind search of the paper (reference) and the two searches frozen for the test split, the cheap one (afc) and the robust one (afr). (a) The bench (validation split of the base set). (b) Distance changing at every image by 5 %. (c) Strong shot noise (full well of 500 electrons). (d) Narrow illumination beam.
